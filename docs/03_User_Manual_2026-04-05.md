## 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Get an **invite code** from your team admin.
2. Go to the app URL or use an invite link (`https://vanto-zazi-bloom.lovable.app/auth?invite=XXXXXX`).
3. Click **"Don't have an account? Sign up"**.
4. Fill in: **email**, **password**, **display name**, and **invite code**.
5. Click **Sign Up**.
6. Check your email and **verify your address** by clicking the confirmation link.
7. Return to the app and **sign in**.

### Signing In
1. Go to the app URL.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

### Forgot Password
1. Click **"Forgot password?"** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your email.
3. Click the reset link in your inbox.
4. Set a new password.

## 2. Installing the App (PWA)

Vanto Zazi can be installed as a standalone app on your device.

### Desktop (Chrome)
1. When you visit the app, an **"Install App"** banner appears at the top-right.
2. Click **Install** to add it to your desktop.
3. The app opens in its own window without browser bars.

### Android (Chrome)
1. A banner appears at the bottom of the screen: **"Install Vanto Zazi"**.
2. Tap **Install** to add it to your home screen.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. A guidance banner appears: **"Tap Share → Add to Home Screen"**.
2. Follow the instructions to add the app icon.

### After Installing
- The install banner hides automatically.
- If you dismiss it, it won't reappear for 7 days.
- The app works offline with a banner indicating limited connectivity.

## 4. Contacts

The core of Vanto Zazi — your complete contact database.

### Viewing & Searching
- Search by name, phone, or email
- Click **Columns** to show/hide fields
- Filter by: Lead Temperature, Registration Status, Lead Type, Focus Area, Lead Path

### Adding a Contact
1. Click **+ Add Contact**.
2. Fill in details (name is required).
3. System checks for duplicates by phone/email.
4. Click **Save**.

### Contact Drawer
Click any contact to open a slide-out panel with:
- All contact fields
- Activity timeline
- Quick actions: WhatsApp, copy phone, AI message, log activity, edit
- Message template picker

### Bulk Actions
- Select contacts with checkboxes
- Click **Delete Selected** to remove

## 5. Activities

Track every interaction with your prospects.

### Activity Timeline
Grouped by date with type icons, contact names, summaries, and timestamps.

### Activity Goals
Set daily targets for calls, emails, and WhatsApp messages. Track progress with visual indicators.

Click **✨ AI Insights** for pattern analysis and improvement suggestions.

## 18. FAQ & Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Invalid invite code" | Get a valid, unused code from your admin |
| Can't sign in after signup | Check email for verification link |
| Duplicates appearing | Go to Duplicates page and merge them |
| Smart Paste timed out | Paste smaller amounts of data |
| Tester Dashboard not visible | Admin-only — contact your administrator |
| AI says "Failed to send" | Check internet connection, refresh page |
| How to export data? | Import/Export → Export section |
| Can I undo a merge? | No — merges are permanent (logged for audit) |
| PWA install not showing | Ensure you're on HTTPS and using Chrome. On iOS use Share → Add to Home Screen |
